How Flat Rate Rides Work

What "flat rate" and "upfront base rate" mean at Green Ride.

The Simple Version

For listed routes, Green Ride shows the base rate before the trip.

A traditional metered fare changes as time and distance accumulate. A published flat or upfront base rate is different: the base amount for the listed route is shown in advance.

That does not mean every possible charge is included. Depending on the trip, applicable gratuity, tolls, parking, waiting time, stops, fuel surcharges, special-event pricing or other charges may be additional.

How to Use Green Ride Flat Rates

1. Choose your pickup area.
2. Choose your destination.
3. Review the published base rate.
4. Call Green Ride to confirm availability and final charges.

Why Upfront Pricing Helps

  • You can review the listed base rate before travel.
  • You can compare routes without waiting until the end of the ride.
  • You can call and confirm additional charges before booking.
  • You have a published reference instead of relying only on a verbal estimate.
