Flat Rate, Upfront Rate, and Metered Fare

Flat rate / upfront base rate: the listed base price for a defined route is shown before travel.

Metered fare: the base fare changes with time and/or distance while the trip is underway.

Green Ride uses published upfront base rates for many listed routes. Additional charges can still apply where stated in Green Ride policies, which is why we ask customers to call and confirm the final trip details before travel.

Flat Rate Ride FAQ

What is a flat rate ride?

For listed Green Ride routes, the published upfront base rate is shown before travel rather than calculated from a running taxi meter.

Does Green Ride use surge pricing on these published rates?

Published base rates are shown in advance. Special-event pricing and other applicable charges may still apply, so call Green Ride to confirm the final rate before travel.

Are gratuity, tolls, parking and waiting included?

Unless specifically stated otherwise, applicable gratuity, tolls, parking, waiting time, stops, fuel surcharges, special-event pricing and other charges may be additional.

What San Diego areas are covered?

The current local-rate tool includes pickup areas such as Airport, Downtown, Hotel Circle, Mission Valley, Pacific Beach, Old Town, Shelter Island and Sports Arena, with many listed destinations.

How do I reserve?

Choose your route, review the published base rate, then call 619-200-2060 or 800-893-7210 to confirm availability and the final trip details.
